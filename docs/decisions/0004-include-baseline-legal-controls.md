# 4. Include the Baseline's legal controls

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Amends:** [0002](0002-scope-of-initial-version.md)

## Context

The initial scope left licensing and legal questions out. The OSPS Baseline, adopted in
0003, contains a Legal category (OSPS-LE: contributor right to commit, open source
licences, licence files in well-known locations).

## Decision

Include the OSPS-LE controls in this version. Licensing and legal questions beyond those
controls stay out of scope.

## Consequences

- Guidance covers every Baseline category, so no control has to be marked as skipped.
- The guidance states what the controls require and does not give legal advice.
