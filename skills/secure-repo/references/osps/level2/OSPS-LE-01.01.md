# OSPS-LE-01.01

- **Control:** OSPS-LE-01, Require Code Contributors to Assert Right to Commit (Legal)
- **Level:** 2+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-le-0101), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.
>
> This guidance says what the controls require. It is not legal advice; choosing a licence
> or a contributor agreement is the human's decision.

- **Requires:** the version control system makes every code contributor assert, on every
  commit, that they are legally entitled to contribute it.
- **Meet it:** the Baseline accepts a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off
  enforced by a status check, or a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). It notes some
  platforms may cover this in their terms of service, and that long histories from before
  adoption may not be fixable retroactively.
- **Verify:** check the mechanism and its enforcement (a required status check, not only
  a policy). A platform setting that covers only commits made in its web interface does
  not cover the rest.
