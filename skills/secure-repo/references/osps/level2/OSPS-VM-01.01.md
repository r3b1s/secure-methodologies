# OSPS-VM-01.01

- **Control:** OSPS-VM-01, Publish Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Policy (Vulnerability Management)
- **Level:** 2+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-vm-0101), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** a coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy with a clear response
  timeframe.
- **Meet it:** a `SECURITY.md` at the repository root saying how to report, how reports
  are handled and how quickly the project will respond. The timeframe is the human's
  commitment to make. Start from the [SECURITY.md template](../../../assets/SECURITY.md.template).
- **Verify:** read it and check the reporting route works.
