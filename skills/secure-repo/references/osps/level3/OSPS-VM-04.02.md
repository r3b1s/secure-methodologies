# OSPS-VM-04.02

- **Control:** OSPS-VM-04, Publish Discovered Vulnerabilities (Vulnerability Management)
- **Level:** 3+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-vm-0402), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** vulnerabilities in components that do not affect the project are recorded
  in a VEX document explaining why they are not exploitable.
- **Meet it:** maintain a VEX feed with the assessment and any mitigation for each such
  vulnerability. The format and tooling are the human's choice.
- **Verify:** compare the VEX statements with the known vulnerabilities in the project's
  dependencies.
