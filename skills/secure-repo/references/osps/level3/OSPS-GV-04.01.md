# OSPS-GV-04.01

- **Control:** OSPS-GV-04, Require Formal Review of Permission Grants (Governance)
- **Level:** 3+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-gv-0401), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** a policy that collaborators are reviewed before being granted escalated
  permissions to sensitive resources.
- **Meet it:** publish an enforceable policy for vetting before granting merge rights or
  secret access, ideally including confirmation of identity.
- **Verify:** read the policy and check recent permission grants against it.
