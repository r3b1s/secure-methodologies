# OSPS-BR-07.02

- **Control:** OSPS-BR-07, Secure Secrets and Credentials (Build and Release)
- **Level:** 3+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-br-0702), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** a documented policy for managing the project's secrets and credentials:
  storage, access and rotation.
- **Meet it:** document where secrets live, who can reach them, and when and how they are
  rotated.
- **Verify:** read the policy and compare it with how secrets are actually configured.
