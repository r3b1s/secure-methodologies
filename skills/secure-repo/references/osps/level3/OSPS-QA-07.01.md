# OSPS-QA-07.01

- **Control:** OSPS-QA-07, Require Merge Approvals (Quality)
- **Level:** 3+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-qa-0701), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** at least one human approval from someone other than the author before a
  change merges into the primary branch.
- **Meet it:** require at least one approving review in the branch protection or ruleset.
- **Verify:** inspect the rule, including bypass permissions, and recent merges.
