- **Control:** OSPS-GV-03, Publish Contribution Guide (Governance)
- **Level:** 1+

> Summarised in this skill's own words.

- **Requires:** an explanation of how to contribute, or a clear statement that public
  contributions are not accepted.
- **Meet it:** a `CONTRIBUTING.md` (or `CONTRIBUTING/` directory) describing the process,
  or that statement.
- **Verify:** read it.
