# OSPS-LE-02.01

- **Control:** OSPS-LE-02, Ensure Project Licenses are Fully Open Source (Legal)
- **Level:** 1+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-le-0201), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.
>
> This guidance says what the controls require. It is not legal advice; choosing a licence
> or a contributor agreement is the human's decision.

- **Requires:** the source code licence meets the OSI Open Source Definition or the FSF
  Free Software Definition.
- **Meet it:** use a licence approved by the OSI or recognised as free by the FSF. The
  Baseline also accepts a public-domain dedication with no other encumbrances.
- **Verify:** identify the licence (an SPDX identifier helps) and check it against the
  OSI or FSF list.
