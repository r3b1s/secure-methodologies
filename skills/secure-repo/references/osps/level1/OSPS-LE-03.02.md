# OSPS-LE-03.02

- **Control:** OSPS-LE-03, Maintain and Release Licenses in a Well Known Location (Legal)
- **Level:** 1+
- **Exact text:** [OSPS Baseline v2026.08.28](https://baseline.openssf.org/versions/2026-08-28#osps-le-0302), the authority

> Summarised in this skill's own words.
>
> This guidance says what the controls require. It is not legal advice; choosing a licence
> or a contributor agreement is the human's decision.

- **Requires:** the licence of released assets is included in the released source, or in
  a `LICENSE` file, `COPYING` file or `LICENSE/` directory next to the assets.
- **Meet it:** include the licence in source archives and alongside binary assets.
- **Verify:** inspect recent releases. Not applicable until the project releases assets.
